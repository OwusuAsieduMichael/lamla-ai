# Pitch notes

LAMLA AI is a student-centered academic intelligence workspace for a defined KNUST Computer Science pilot.

## What to show

- Official scope: KNUST → College of Science → Faculty of Physical and Computational Sciences → Department of Computer Science → BSc Computer Science
- Ask, Sources, Vision, Voice, Learn, and Questions as one engine
- Refusal when authorized sources are missing
- No invented course catalog, lecture notes, or past papers

## What not to claim

- That LAMLA already knows the KNUST syllabus
- That vision can read a random photo as a lecture
- That voice invents a transcript or an answer
- That recommendations exist without authorized materials

## Demo script

1. Open Home and read the pilot path.
2. Ask a KNUST syllabus question. The app should refuse.
3. Open Sources, Learn, and Questions. They should be empty until granted materials exist.
4. Explain that answers begin only after authorized collection, ingestion, and retrieval.

The product story is source-grounded assistance, not a generic chatbot.
